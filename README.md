A deadpan weather forecast for the invented city of Dranmoor, written as a single line a straight-faced TV meteorologist could read off the map: fog at knee height that refuses to lift without an apology, a high of 12, a low of mild regret, and visibility of one arm's length.

`main.py` writes the forecast to `/output/entry.txt` (one line, 151 characters, within the 1–160 character gate).
